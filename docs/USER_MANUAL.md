# User Manual

## Purpose

Planning to use AI in your organization? AI Governance Model Lab helps you assess AI use cases, understand potential governance risks, and explore similar examples before making implementation decisions. The application is designed to support transparency and informed decision-making—it does not provide legal advice or regulatory certification.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Getting Started

### Step 1 --- Describe the AI Use Case

Enter a short description of the AI system or scenario you would like to
assess.

**Example**

``` text
An AI system analyzes job applications and recommends candidates for interviews.
```

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Step 2 --- Classify the Use Case

Select **Classify Use Case**.

The application analyzes the description and generates a governance
assessment.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Step 3 — Review the Results

The assessment provides three key pieces of information:

- **Risk Category** – Shows the predicted governance risk of your AI use case.
- **Confidence Score** – Indicates how certain the AI model is about the prediction.
- **Similar Cases** – Displays comparable AI use cases to help you understand the assessment and identify similar governance situations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Good Practices

For the best results:

- Clearly describe the purpose of the AI system.
- Include enough information for the AI to understand the use case.
- Do not submit confidential, personal, or sensitive information.
- Use the assessment as guidance to support discussions and decision-making, not as a legal or regulatory opinion.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Digital Trust

This application publishes a **Digital Trust Profile** describing its AI
usage, governance, privacy, and transparency characteristics.

The machine-readable profile is available at:

``` text
/.well-known/digital-trust.json
```

## Intended Audience

AI Governance Model Lab is intended for:

-   Organizations exploring AI adoption
-   AI governance professionals
-   Compliance and risk teams
-   Technical architects
-   Students and researchers

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Important Information

The assessment provided by this application is intended to support your understanding of AI governance.

Please remember:

- The results are guidance, not legal advice.
- The application does not perform formal compliance or conformity assessments.
- Always combine the assessment with human judgment and, where appropriate, professional legal or regulatory advice.
